Method & Confidence

This dashboard separates recorded inputs from model-derived estimates. Salary, benefits, tax/insurance, overhead, absence, management hours, direct damage and project profit are user-entered values. Presenteeism, talent waste, turnover/replacement, knowledge loss and learning-curve costs are estimates produced by the calculator model. Use the results as a management decision-support indicator, not as audited accounting cost. For stronger accuracy, calibrate coefficients against your company's historical HR and project data.
